Support

Frequently asked questions and how to get help.

How do I add a reminder or bill?

Open the app, tap the smart input at the top, and type something like “Car insurance due March 12” or “Netflix $15 monthly”. You can also use the “Add item” flow to set a title, due date, and how many days before you want to be reminded.

How do I import transactions from a statement?

On the Spend tab, use “Pick file” to choose a PDF, CSV, or text file from your device. The app will try to extract transaction lines and add them. You can also paste lines of text (e.g. “Starbucks $5.50”) and use “Analyze & add from text” if that option is available in your version.

Where is my data stored?

Reminders, transactions, and settings can be synced to the cloud if you sign in with an account. Subscriptions are stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I clear subscriptions or reset data?

Open Settings in the app, go to the Data section, and use “Clear subscriptions” to remove all subscriptions, or “Clear all data” to delete everything. “Reset demo data” replaces your data with sample items and transactions.

Contact

For bugs, feature requests, or other questions, please email us at getklovio@gmail.com. We’ll do our best to respond within a few days.
