Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

Klario (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is designed to keep your reminders, spending, and subscriptions in one place. We care about your privacy and aim to store only what’s needed to run the app and, if you choose, to sync your data to the cloud.

Data we collect and use

  • Account data. If you sign in (e.g. with email and password), we store your account credentials and a unique identifier so you can sync data across devices.
  • Synced content. When you sign in, items (reminders, bills), transactions, and app settings may be stored on our servers so you can access them from other devices. This data is tied to your account.
  • Subscriptions. Subscription lists are stored only on your device and are not synced to our servers.
  • Import and processing. If you use file or PDF import, the file content may be sent to our backend or a third-party service to extract text or transactions. We do not use this content for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Data we do not sell

We do not sell your personal data or synced content to third parties for advertising or marketing.

Security

We use industry-standard practices (including encryption in transit and, where applicable, at rest) to protect your account and synced data. You are responsible for keeping your sign-in credentials secure.

Your choices

  • You can use the app without signing in; in that case, all data stays on your device.
  • You can sign out or delete your account; we will delete or anonymize your account and synced data in line with our retention policy.
  • You can clear all data or reset demo data from the app’s Settings at any time.

Children

Klario is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have received such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or our practices can be sent to getklario@gmail.com.
